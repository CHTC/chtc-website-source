# Students in Courses

If your instructor has already applied for CHTC class accounts, wait for an email from a facilitator or your instructor about login details.

If your instructor has not applied for CHTC class accounts, but you would like to use CHTC resources for course work, **your instructor should email us at [chtc@cs.wisc.edu](mailto:chtc@cs.wisc.edu).**
