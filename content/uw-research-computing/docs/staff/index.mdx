Internal documentation for people who work on CHTC's website and services. These pages are
kept out of search engines, the sitemap and the LLM export, but they are on the public site:
do not put anything here that should stay private.

## Docs

- [Writing documentation](/uw-research-computing/staff/writing-documentation/): how the guides
  on this site are written, with every available building block and how it renders.
- [Doc components](/uw-research-computing/staff/doc-components/): every component the guides
  use, with its options and one example.
- [Adding news articles](/uw-research-computing/staff/adding-news-articles/): publishing an
  article through a preview branch and a pull request.
- [Schedule calendar](/uw-research-computing/staff/schedule-calendar/): recording your schedule
  and days off.
