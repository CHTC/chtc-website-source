Beyond the CHTC GPU Lab, your jobs can run on more GPUs. These GPUs do not have the GPU Lab's
[time limits and job limits](/uw-research-computing/gpu-jobs/quickstart/#gpu-job-limits), but
jobs on them may be interrupted. Jobs that run in a few hours or less, or that
[checkpoint](/uw-research-computing/gpu-jobs/checkpointing/), are a good fit.

All GPU jobs need `request_gpus` in their submit file, even if they are not using the GPU Lab.

# Research group GPUs (backfill)

Certain GPU servers in CHTC are prioritized for the research groups that own them, but run other
jobs when their owners are not using them. Jobs running on these servers can be interrupted.
For shorter jobs, or jobs that checkpoint, this is not a drawback, and these servers add
capacity.

We recommend that all GPU jobs opt in. Add this line to your submit file:

```condor
+is_resumable = true
```

# Campus pools and the OSPool

CHTC is a member of the [OSG Consortium](https://osg-htc.org/) and can use GPUs in the
[OSPool](https://osg-htc.org/about/open_science_pool/). CHTC is also a member of the UW Grid, a
campus computing network where groups share computing capacity, including idle GPUs.

See [Scale beyond local HTC capacity](/uw-research-computing/scaling-htc/) to find out whether
your jobs are good candidates for the UW Grid or the OSPool.
