## What to expect

A facilitator will follow up to arrange a time to meet, usually within 2-3 business days. In
that conversation we will learn about your work, describe the computing capacity and services
available to UW-Madison researchers and educators at no cost, and recommend an approach.

## Other ways to reach us

* **Ready for an account already?** Go straight to the [account request](form.html).
* **Already a CHTC user with a technical question?** Email [chtc@cs.wisc.edu](mailto:chtc@cs.wisc.edu) or see [Get Help](get-help.html) for support options.
* **Prefer to drop in?** Join our virtual [office hours](get-help.html#office-hours).
* **Faculty with broader questions** about computing for your group, or about startup and recruitment needs, can find the right contact on [Talk to Us](/talk-to-us.html).
